# Product Brief

## Problem

Collections teams need to recover overdue or soon-to-breach payments without manually chasing every borrower. Human agents spend too much time on repetitive reminders, simple negotiation, and follow-up scheduling.

## Solution

SettleWise is an AI collections demo app. It calls borrowers, negotiates repayment options by voice, sends mock payment links over SMS, schedules SMS reminders, and updates borrower memory.

## Target User

- Hackathon judges.
- Fintech/lending teams evaluating an AI collections workflow.
- Collections ops teams who want to see how much repetitive work can be automated.

## Borrower Experience

The borrower should feel like they are dealing with a professional, respectful collections representative:

- Clear explanation of amount due and deadline.
- No shame, threats, or pressure tactics.
- Practical negotiation around what can be paid today.
- Easy SMS payment link or payment confirmation flow.
- Flexible scheduling around salary or cash-flow dates.
- Fast escalation when there is a dispute, hardship, or confusion.

## Product Goals

- Demonstrate repayment negotiation before breach.
- Show a clean borrower/contact dashboard.
- Simulate agent-driven voice outreach and SMS follow-up.
- Show memory-driven reasoning like salary dates and preferred contact times.
- Generate mock payment links and payment outcomes.
- Make the product feel real enough to pitch.

## Non-Goals

- No production integrations unless easy.
- No deep legal workflow.
- No real payment processing.
- No real outbound calling in the demo.

## Positioning

SettleWise should be positioned as an AI collections agent that turns borrower conversations into payment outcomes.
